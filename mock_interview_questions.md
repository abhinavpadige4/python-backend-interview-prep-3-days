## Preparation Tips

### Technical Preparation
1. Practice coding problems daily on platforms like LeetCode
2. Review core Python concepts and idioms
3. Study database concepts and SQL thoroughly
4. Practice system design problems regularly
5. Review common design patterns and when to use them
6. Practice explaining your thought process aloud
7. Work on timing yourself for coding problems
8. Review your past projects and be ready to discuss them
9. Prepare questions to ask the interviewer
10. Do mock interviews with friends or use online platforms

### Day-of-Interview Tips
1. Get a good night's sleep before the interview
2. Eat a healthy breakfast
3. Arrive early (or log in early for virtual interviews)
4. Dress appropriately for the company culture
5. Bring copies of your resume and any relevant materials
6. Have a glass of water nearby
7. Take deep breaths to calm nerves
8. Listen carefully to questions and ask for clarification if needed
9. Think before speaking - it's okay to take a moment
10. Be honest about what you don't know and show willingness to learn
11. Show enthusiasm for the role and company
12. Thank the interviewer for their time at the end

### Follow-up
1. Send a thank-you email within 24 hours
2. Reference specific topics discussed in the interview
3. Reiterate your interest in the position
4. Address any concerns that came up during the interview
5. Keep it concise and professional
